# Yapnest 1.0 (12)

## Added

- Settings > Encryption Keys has Reset Encryption for This Device. It
  gives this device a new key and takes the old one off your account, so
  everyone's apps see it as a new device and accept it as they would a new
  phone. It's for when people stopped being able to read you after the
  device's key changed, as an older build installed over a newer one
  could do: their apps held on to the old key, and people who aren't your
  contacts never heard of the new one. Your chats stay.

## Changed

- On the phone and iPad, the bar at the foot of the chat list is bigger,
  with its three buttons further apart.
- Settings moved to the top left of the chat list, and the bar at its
  foot has a Search button in its place, which opens the search field
  even when you've scrolled down.

## Fixed

- Sharing a file from another app keeps its name, and sharing a picture
  that isn't a file works.
- Removing devices, loading the banned list, saving a group and listing
  commands now give the server's reason in plain words, with Retry where
  trying again could help.
- Accepting or declining a contact request, removing a contact or changing
  their nickname while offline is no longer refused: it shows as waiting
  and is sent when you're back online.
- Changing your photo or display name while offline is no longer refused:
  it shows as waiting and is sent when you're back online. If you changed
  it on another device meanwhile, that one is kept and you're told.
- Changing who can see your name and photo while offline now takes effect
  as soon as you're back online, not only after the app is restarted.
- Calling someone just as the app reconnects now waits for the connection
  and for the contact to show as online instead of failing at once, and
  the call button spins meanwhile. When it can't call, the message says why.
- A group's notification setting changed while offline is no longer lost,
  and a group you were removed from while offline isn't joined again.
- On the Mac, scrolling a long chat no longer stutters. Moving the pointer
  over messages redraws only the ones under it.
- A message that reused another one's id, as some bots and a copied
  message in an anonymous group can, is no longer lost on catching up.
- A correction that couldn't be decrypted, arriving before the message it
  corrects, no longer leaves a stray "couldn't be decrypted" message behind.
- A notification could come twice when a push arrived just as the app
  opened.
- Links to large web pages now get a preview, and so do sites on a
  network where one of their addresses can't be reached.
- A chat you read while offline now shows as read on your other devices
  once you're back online, and stops notifying there.
- Blocking or unblocking someone while offline is no longer refused: it
  takes effect at once and is sent to your server when you're back online.
  If the server refuses it, the person is put back and you're told why.
