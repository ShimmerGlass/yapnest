# Yapnest 1.0 (12)

## Added

- Settings > Encryption Keys has Reset Encryption for This Device: a new
  key, which everyone's apps accept as a new device. It's for when people
  can't read you after the device's key changed, as an older build
  installed over a newer one could do. Your chats stay.

## Changed

- *Bold*, _italic_, ~struck~ and `code` show without their markers in
  messages, and the composer shows the styles as you type.
- On the phone and iPad, the bar at the foot of the chat list is bigger,
  with its three buttons further apart.
- Settings moved to the top left of the chat list, and the bar at its
  foot has a Search button in its place, which opens the search field
  even when you've scrolled down.
- A long chat takes less work to update as messages arrive, and opening a
  chat builds its message box faster.

## Fixed

- Changes made offline are no longer refused or lost: accepting,
  declining or removing a contact, their nickname, blocking, your photo,
  display name and who sees them, and a group's notifications. They show
  as waiting and are sent once you're back online. If the server
  refuses one, you're told why.
- If you changed your photo or name on another device meanwhile, that one
  is kept and you're told.
- A group you were removed from while offline isn't joined again.
- A chat read offline shows as read on your other devices once the
  message it was read up to arrives, and stops notifying there.
- Encrypted messages sent right after rejoining a private group wait for
  its member list, so members who are offline can read them too.
- Settings, or your status, changed in quick succession land in order.
- Sharing from another app keeps the file's name, works for pictures that
  aren't files, and says when the share is queued and why.
- A message not encrypted for this device now says so.
- Server errors now give the reason in plain words, with Retry where
  trying again could help.
- Calling while reconnecting waits, and says why when it can't call.
- On the Mac, scrolling a long chat no longer stutters.
- Message bubbles keep to their width. A short chat's first message no
  longer springs back under the bar, and a flick to the newest message
  no longer slows near the end.
- A correction that couldn't be decrypted, arriving before the message it
  corrects, no longer leaves a stray "couldn't be decrypted" message.
- A contact's offline message no longer shows after the messages you sent
  meanwhile.
- A reaction from someone who took a former member's nickname no longer
  stops a group's history from loading.
- A message that reused another one's id, as some bots and a copied
  message in an anonymous group can, is no longer lost on catching up.
- Message pushes now ring before the phone's first unlock after a
  restart, and a notification no longer comes twice.
- Links to large pages, or that failed on a bad network, now get a
  preview.
- A picture too big to show no longer closes the app.
- Notifications keep working after the clock was set back.
- Sound the player can't play, such as WebM, shows as a file, and FLAC
  or AIFF sent without a type plays in the chat.
- Cancel in the share sheet closes it at once, even while a large file
  is being copied.
- A /me message that starts with a quote shows the name before the
  quote, not inside it.
- A message the server put off after a restart is sent again right away.
- You can leave a group offline; your other devices follow once you're
  back online.
- A reaction taken back offline is no longer lost.
- Archived chats can be picked in the share sheet.
- A video or RAW photo whose location can't be removed now says it can't
  be sent.
- Your devices agree on which chats are archived even when their clocks
  differ.
- A group correction from a device not yet checked applies once it is.
- In private groups, "joined" and "left" now mean added to or taken off
  the member list, not going online or offline. Other groups no longer
  show them.
