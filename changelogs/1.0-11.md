# Yapnest 1.0 (11)

## Added

- The birds and leaves behind a chat's messages drift with them as you
  scroll, at a quarter of their speed.
- A group's chat now says "Bob joined the group" and "Bob left the group",
  so someone a server adds quietly to an encrypted group can't read along
  unseen. Private groups always show these lines, others only up to 50
  people.
- Your name, photo, status and account have a Profile screen of their
  own, with Change Password and Sign Out. On the phone and iPad, Profile,
  Settings and New Chat are in a bar at the bottom of the chat list. On
  the Mac, your name in the sidebar opens Profile and the gear opens
  Settings.

## Fixed

- In an encrypted group, a message from someone's new device is only read
  once their own server confirms the device is theirs, so a group's
  server can't pass off a device of its own as a member's. Until then it
  shows as locked.
- Installing an older build over this one and updating again could give
  the device a new encryption key, and messages to it were lost. It now
  keeps the key your contacts know, and never deletes a key it replaces.
- After installing on a new phone, people in your private groups who
  aren't your contacts couldn't send you readable messages until you
  posted there. A new device now introduces itself to each group's
  members, and again, at most twice a day, to someone whose message it
  can't read.
- Your messages in shared groups didn't reach a phone added by someone
  who is no longer your contact until the app restarted. They now do
  within a minute.
- Replying to a link ending like a file name, such as "…/release-1.5",
  quoted it as a file. It now quotes as the link.

## Changed

- Long chats are much lighter on the phone: storing a thousand new
  messages in a chat of 40,000 took over ten seconds and now takes under
  one. Opening, scrolling, unread counts and deleting a chat no longer
  grow with its history, and sending in a large encrypted group takes a
  third of the time.
- A build older than the last one to use your account refuses to open it
  and asks you to update, rather than running older code over your
  messages and keys.
- The diagnostics export says more about encryption keys, to help explain
  messages that can't be decrypted. It holds counts, no addresses.
- With a keyboard, the arrow keys, Tab and Return pick a name after "@" in
  a group, and Escape hides the suggestions.
- Signing in with many contacts, catching up after time offline, and
  receipts in busy groups are lighter.
- GIFs are decoded in the background rather than while the screen is
  drawn, which took a third of the phone's main thread with the picker
  open.
- Scrolling a chat is lighter: each message's edge is drawn by the
  graphics chip, and its parts are measured once rather than three times.
- Opening a chat: its messages slide in with it instead of popping in
  partway through.
- On the Mac, the scroll-to-newest button takes a click anywhere on its
  circle, and no longer stalls from far up a long chat. Scrolling a long
  chat is smoother too: a message's quick actions hide while it scrolls.
