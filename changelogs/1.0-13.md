# Yapnest 1.0 (13)

## Fixed

- Show more no longer leaves bold or italic markers where it cuts a message.
- On iOS 27, the blur under a chat's top bar looks like the system's.
- A moment when the encryption keys can't be read, such as a full disk, no
  longer resets an encrypted chat.
- Mac: a message's quick actions sit at the bubble's inner edge, fade in and
  out, stay up on the way to a button and after an emoji pick, and a short
  message is wide enough for them.
- Mac: GIFs hold still while Yapnest isn't the app in front, and messages
  arriving then show a notification and stay unread.
- Mac: Esc cancels a reply or an edit, and the menu commands no longer act
  on the Welcome screen or behind an open sheet.
- A stranger's message no longer makes Yapnest ask for their picture, which
  told their server you were online.
- Signing out also deletes copies of large GIFs and, on the Mac, of files
  you opened.
- A notification whose messages couldn't be fetched shows "New message"
  instead of nothing, and it goes once the real one is shown.
- A message waiting to be sent when you left a group, were removed or it was
  deleted shows as not sent, instead of going out when you rejoin.
- A group's subject change is tried again when the group is slow to answer.
- A group's history shown on joining no longer credits old messages to whoever
  has that nickname now, or shows your own twice.
- Your own reaction or message no longer shows twice after the history is
  read again.
- VoiceOver says when a message wasn't encrypted or came from an unverified
  device, and in the chat list whether your last message was sent.
- A chat's Call, Video and Search buttons grow with the text size.
- Encrypted files over 200 MB fail with a clear message instead of crashing
  the app; tapping a file over 200 MB says it's too large.
- A file still arriving when its message is retracted is no longer kept.
- After a reconnect, a call answered elsewhere or given up stops ringing.
- Sheets can't be closed while their request runs, and a notification or
  link tapped meanwhile waits for them, so no failure goes unseen.
- Opening the chat that's already open keeps its staged files and reply.
- After a relaunch, editing or deleting a message the server never confirmed
  reaches the others.
- Encrypt New Chats is kept on each device rather than synced, so your
  server can't turn it off.
- A group that stops being private says in the chat that new messages
  there aren't encrypted any more.
- Groups: a change to who sees addresses is picked up at once; a bookmark
  keeps the password and settings other apps stored in it; inviting keeps
  admins' roles, a ban, and the room's password.
- Adding a contact while offline is kept and sent once you're connected.
  Adding someone who asked to add you answers their request.
- In a chat, the archive or a sheet over the chat list, a message for
  another chat shows a notification, and the back button counts what is
  unread elsewhere.
- Links in received messages are lighter in dark mode, and the time and
  ticks on your own messages are brighter, so both are easier to read.
- Maps in shared locations are drawn only once you agree, can be turned off
  in Settings, and wait for a tap from people you haven't added.
