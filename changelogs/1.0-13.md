# Yapnest 1.0 (13)

## Fixed

- A long message cut short with Show more no longer leaves its bold or
  italic markers showing where the cut falls inside them.
- On iOS 27, the blur under a chat's top bar now looks like the system's:
  even, and ending on a thin line at the bar's edge.
- A moment when the app can't read its encryption keys, such as a full
  disk, no longer resets a working encrypted chat with a contact.
- On the Mac, a message's quick actions now appear at the same place every
  time, at the bubble's inner edge, rather than wherever the pointer came in,
  and stay put when the pointer reaches them over the message above. They
  fade in and out, and a short message is drawn wide enough to carry them.
- On the Mac, a message's quick actions no longer stop appearing after a
  reaction picked from Emoji & Symbols, until the chat was scrolled.
- On the Mac, GIFs stop on the frame they're showing when Yapnest's window
  isn't the one in focus, and play again when it is.
- A message from someone you haven't accepted no longer makes Yapnest ask
  for their picture, which told their server you were online.
- Signing out now also deletes the smaller copies of large GIFs and, on the
  Mac, the copies of files you opened.
- A notification whose messages couldn't be fetched in time, such as after
  the password was changed elsewhere, now still shows the server's "New
  message" instead of nothing.
- That "New message" is taken down once the real message has been shown or
  the app is opened, and a message the notification service ran out of time
  for is no longer lost.
- A message that was still waiting to be sent when you left a group, were
  removed from it or it was deleted now shows as not sent, with the reason,
  instead of a clock that never ends and a send when you join again. Retry
  sends it.
- Changing a group's subject now tries again when the group is slow to
  answer, instead of showing a timeout after the first try.
- VoiceOver now says when a message wasn't encrypted, or came from a device
  that isn't verified.
- On the Mac, Settings or New Chat chosen from the menu while the Welcome
  screen is up no longer opens later by itself or sticks.
- On the Mac, the menu's Find, Edit Last Message, New Chat and Settings no
  longer act while Profile, Settings, New Chat or Join Group is open.
- VoiceOver now says in the chat list that your last message is yours, and
  whether it was sent, delivered, read or not sent.
- Call, Video and Search in a chat's settings grow with the text size and
  stack when they no longer fit in a row.
- On the Mac, Esc cancels a reply or an edit in the message field.
- A file over 200 MB sent in an encrypted chat now fails with a clear
  message, instead of crashing the app and being retried at every launch.
