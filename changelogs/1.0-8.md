# Yapnest 1.0 (8)

## New

- Chats have a faint background of low-poly mauve branches, with
  wireframe Yapnest birds perched on them, and a few branches reach into
  the top of the chat list.
- On the Mac, the Cancel and confirm buttons at the top of Edit Group and
  the other sheets are icons, like New Chat's, with their names as
  tooltips.
- The Last Seen setting is now Activity Status. With it on, your contacts
  also see you as away once you leave Yapnest on iPhone or iPad, rather
  than online for as long as the server keeps your session, and as you
  chose again when you come back. Do not disturb and your status message
  are kept.

## Fixed

- A chat's Call button no longer blinks off and back on as the chat
  opens, or when Yapnest reconnects. Calling someone who has just gone
  offline says they can't take a call right now.
- In the chat list, a chat's name stays in the same place whether its last
  message takes one line or two, and the time and unread badge are spaced
  evenly down the right of the row.
- On the Mac, the message you're replying to is shown at its own width
  above the message field, with its ✕ right beside it, instead of the ✕
  sitting halfway across a wide window.
- A status changed while Yapnest was reconnecting now reaches your
  contacts once it's back.
