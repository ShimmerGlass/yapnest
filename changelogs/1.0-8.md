# Yapnest 1.0 (8)

## New

- Chats have a faint background of low-poly mauve branches, with
  wireframe Yapnest birds perched on them, and a few branches reach into
  the top of the chat list.
- On the Mac, the Cancel and confirm buttons at the top of Edit Group and
  the other sheets are icons, like New Chat's, with their names as
  tooltips.
- The Last Seen setting is now Activity Status. With it on, your contacts
  also see you as away once you leave Yapnest on iPhone or iPad, rather
  than online for as long as the server keeps your session, and as you
  chose again when you come back. On the Mac, they see you as away once
  it's been left untouched for 10 minutes or locked, and your last seen
  counts from when you last used the Mac rather than Yapnest. Do not
  disturb and your status message are kept.

## Fixed

- A chat's Call button no longer blinks off and back on as the chat
  opens, or when Yapnest reconnects. Calling someone who has just gone
  offline says they can't take a call right now.
- In the chat list, a chat's name stays in the same place whether its last
  message takes one line or two, and the time and unread badge are spaced
  evenly down the right of the row.
- On the Mac, the message you're replying to is shown at its own width
  above the message field, with its ✕ right beside it, instead of the ✕
  sitting halfway across a wide window.
- A status changed while Yapnest was reconnecting now reaches your
  contacts once it's back.
- Your encryption key stays on your device: it's no longer in iCloud or
  computer backups. A phone restored from a backup sets up encryption as
  a new device, and keeps what you decided about your contacts' devices.
- Your messages database, attachments, avatars, link previews and logs
  say outright that they stay locked after a restart until you first
  unlock your phone, rather than rely on iOS's default.
- Settings > Open source licences now says which versions of the AGPL
  Yapnest is under: version 3 or any later one.
- When the connection drops just as Yapnest is catching up on messages it
  missed, it no longer shows as up to date for a moment, and it fetches
  those messages once it's back, rather than leaving them out.
- On the iPhone, when you've scrolled up to older messages, photos and
  link previews that finish loading further down no longer move the
  messages you're reading. They show once you're back at the newest
  message.
- On macOS 15, a chat's name and picture at the top of the window are no
  longer cut in half by the title bar.
