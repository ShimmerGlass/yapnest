# Yapnest 1.0 (10)

## Added

- Hide Screen in App Switcher (iPhone and iPad, Settings, off by default):
  your chats are covered in the app switcher, Control Center and system
  prompts.
- A group that notifies for every message can be limited to one
  notification every 5 or 15 minutes or an hour. Mentions and replies to
  you always notify.
- Privacy settings: Show My Name and Photo to Everyone (off: only your
  contacts see them), Download Pictures from Group Members, Always Relay
  Calls (hides your IP address from who you call) and Include Messages in
  Backups.
- In encrypted chats, calls are checked through OMEMO, as Conversations
  and Dino do, so a server can't listen in. The call screen says whether a
  trusted device vouched for it.
- Group invitations wait for your answer instead of joining at once.
- A new device on your account shows a warning with its fingerprint; a
  new device of a contact's shows a line in the chat.

## Changed

- On the Mac, your server is told you are away while the screen is
  locked, and the Dock shows a dot for unread messages in quiet groups.
  Quick actions show only over the message itself.
- New backgrounds: birds and leaves behind chats. Settings icons are
  mauve.
- Pictures sent as files lose their camera details too.
- Calls, files, receipts, typing and invitations come only from contacts
  and people you approved, and a stranger's photo is fetched only once you
  accept their request.
- Signing out deletes the diagnostic log, which now says why a message
  couldn't be decrypted, without saying who sent it.
- Signing in is harder to intercept: the token is tied to the
  connection, and a weaker sign-in than before is refused.
- On the Mac, only pictures, videos, sound and PDFs open in their app, and
  a file's kind is judged from its name.
- Files upload only over HTTPS, never to your local network. Pictures that
  fail to download wait for a tap, and wait during Low Data Mode.
- Long chats open more smoothly, big groups join faster, and returning
  from Control Center no longer checks every group again.
- The GIF picker asks before it first contacts KLIPY (US).

## Fixed

- Encryption: the signed prekey is replaced weekly (your fingerprint
  stays), used one-time keys are no longer published again, devices with
  ids from 2,147,483,648 up are no longer dropped, and a flooded device
  list is cut to 64.
- Connecting: an update that changes nothing no longer restarts a
  sign-in, and an attempt cut short by a network change can still pick up
  where the last connection left off.
- Battery and processor: a core no longer stays busy after a contact goes
  offline, and GIFs, typing and paused voice messages cost less. GIFs stop
  in the background and, on the Mac, while it is locked or covered.
- Groups: members can no longer change the group's name or picture, put
  words in someone else's mouth in the history, or edit a former
  nickname holder's messages. A group you left stops receiving messages,
  new ejabberd groups don't turn on room logging, and a server's error
  shows as plain text.
- Blocked people who still get through receive no receipts or typing,
  can't ring you and get no pictures downloaded.
- A reply after turning encryption off no longer quotes the encrypted
  text, and a quote of an unknown message shows no name the sender chose.
- Crafted or broken data no longer crashes or slows Yapnest: server
  redirects, slow or huge link previews, endless group histories, heavy
  pictures, oversized GIFs, and floods of status updates. Another account
  can no longer hide the call buttons by lying about an app.
- Names and photos from people you haven't added are ignored. A
  contact's picture shows when you first open their chat.
- On the Mac, messages arriving while the chat can't be seen stay unread.
- Catching up no longer ends "typing…" for what a contact writes now,
  maps are kept between launches, and hanging up as the connection
  returns is no longer lost.
