# Yapnest 1.0 (10)

## Added

- A group that notifies you for every message can now be limited to one
  notification every 5 minutes, 15 minutes or hour, in the group's
  settings under Notifications. The messages in between arrive silently
  and still count in the badge, and the notification already shown is
  updated to say how many messages came in. A message that mentions you,
  says your nickname or replies to yours always notifies.
- New privacy settings:
  - Show My Name and Photo to Everyone, off by default: only your
    contacts see your name and photo, not people you haven't added, even
    in your groups. Your account is switched over the next time it
    connects.
  - Download Pictures from Group Members, on by default: turn it off to
    tap pictures from group members you haven't added before they
    download, as downloading shows your IP address to their server.
  - Always Relay Calls, off by default: calls go only through your
    server's relay, hiding your IP address from the person you call. If
    your server has none, a call is refused with an explanation before
    either phone rings.
  - Include Messages in Backups, on by default: turn it off to keep this
    device's messages and files out of iCloud and computer backups. A
    restored device then gets its history back from the server, but
    can't read encrypted messages from before.
- Invitations to a group now wait for your answer, with a notification,
  instead of joining the group straight away.

## Changed

- On the Mac, your server is told you are away while the screen is locked
  and back when you unlock it, so it holds back chat noise for a Mac
  nobody is looking at. Notifications and everything else are unchanged.
- On the Mac, a message's quick actions now show only while the pointer
  is on the message itself, not anywhere across its row, and the row no
  longer lights up under the pointer.
- On the Mac, the Dock icon shows a dot when only groups that don't
  notify you have unread messages, like their dot in the chat list. A
  count of messages that notify still takes its place.
- The chat list no longer draws branches and birds behind the chats; its
  soft colour wash stays.
- Behind a chat's messages, the big branches gave way to small Yapnest
  birds and leaves of a few kinds, spread evenly.
- In Settings and a chat's or group's settings, every row's icon is now
  mauve and its text black or white, or red for one that removes
  something. A chat's Call, Video and Search buttons sit a little further
  from its name and closer to the settings below.
- A picture sent as a file now loses its camera details (make, model,
  serial, time taken, software, owner) as well as its location.
- Someone's photo is fetched only once you accept their contact request,
  so a stranger's server doesn't learn you're online from the request.
- Signing out also deletes the diagnostic log, and the next account you
  sign in gets a new name for this device on its server.
- Calls, files, receipts, typing and group invitations now come only from
  your contacts and people you approved, not from anyone who happens to
  have your address. Pictures download by themselves only for them, and
  for members of your private groups unless you turn that off.
- On the Mac, only pictures, videos, sound and PDFs received as files
  open in their app; anything else opens in a preview. A file's kind is
  now judged from its name, never from what the sender says it is, and
  invisible characters that can disguise a file's name are removed.
- Signing in is harder to intercept. Where your server allows it, the
  token that signs you in without your password is tied to the
  connection, so it can't be reused if someone copies it, and Yapnest
  now refuses to sign in a weaker way than your account has before. If
  that happens, it says so instead of sending your password, as someone
  may be intercepting the connection; signing out and in again accepts
  the change.
- Pictures that download by themselves no longer try again and again
  when the link has expired or the file can't be read: they wait for you
  to tap them, and after a few failed tries for other reasons too. They
  also wait while Low Data Mode is on.
- Coming back to Yapnest after a few seconds away, such as from Control
  Center or a notification, no longer checks every group again, and
  groups set to never notify are no longer read in the background when
  a notification arrives.
- A file is only uploaded to an address on the internet over HTTPS,
  never to one on your own network, whatever your server answers.

## Fixed

- On the Mac especially, Yapnest no longer keeps a processor core busy
  after a contact goes offline, until another contact's availability
  changes.
- Someone you blocked whose messages still get through, as on Openfire
  right after blocking, no longer gets receipts or typing from you, can't
  ring you and has no pictures downloaded.
- A message your contact wrote earlier, read back from the server when
  catching up, no longer ends the "typing…" shown for what they are
  writing now.
- The message list, the chat list and the Mac's text selection take less
  work, and the battery less, while someone types to you, a GIF plays, a
  voice message is paused or a chat's settings are open. A GIF stops
  playing while Yapnest is in the background, and a paused voice message
  lets music from other apps go on.
- A GIF you send is only fetched over HTTPS and only up to 15 MB.
- A server's redirect with a broken address, or one slipped in before the
  connection is encrypted, can no longer crash Yapnest on every launch.
- A group member can no longer change or remove a group's picture or name
  for everyone through a crafted message.
- A group member can no longer make an older message in a group's
  history appear to come from someone else, or be read as theirs.
- An error a group's server sends is shown as plain text, so it can't
  carry a link.
- Another account can no longer hide the call buttons for everyone using
  a given app by lying about what that app supports.
- On the Mac, messages arriving in the open chat while its window can't
  be seen, such as with the screen locked or the window on another
  desktop, now stay unread until you see them.
- New private groups on ejabberd servers no longer turn on room
  logging, which can publish a group's conversation as a web page.
- A link to a page made to slow down its preview no longer holds the app
  up.
- A group whose server keeps saying there are older messages, or sends
  the same page again and again, no longer keeps Yapnest downloading its
  history without end.
- Replying to a message from an encrypted chat after turning encryption
  off no longer sends that message's text unencrypted: the reply is sent
  without the quote.
- A reply to a message Yapnest doesn't have no longer shows a name that
  the sender picked: the quote appears without one.
- Names and photos pushed by people you haven't added and have no chat
  with are ignored.
- Huge pictures behind a link preview are no longer opened, and a link
  whose page couldn't be reached is tried again after an hour rather than
  every time its message comes into view.
- Contacts' photos are asked for less often, and a group you left no
  longer receives new messages.
- Someone flooding you with status updates from many devices or
  addresses can no longer slow Yapnest down or fill its memory, and a
  photo announced that way is only fetched from people you added or
  approved.
