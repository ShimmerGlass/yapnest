# Yapnest 1.0 (10)

## Added

- A group that notifies you for every message can now be limited to one
  notification every 5 minutes, 15 minutes or hour, in the group's
  settings under Notifications. The messages in between arrive silently
  and still count in the badge, and the notification already shown is
  updated to say how many messages came in. A message that mentions you,
  says your nickname or replies to yours always notifies.
- New privacy settings:
  - Show My Name and Photo to Everyone, off by default: only your
    contacts see your name and photo, not people you haven't added, even
    in your groups. Your account is switched over the next time it
    connects.
  - Download Pictures from Group Members, on by default: turn it off to
    tap pictures from group members you haven't added before they
    download, as downloading shows your IP address to their server.
  - Always Relay Calls, off by default: calls go only through your
    server's relay, hiding your IP address from the person you call. If
    your server has none, a call is refused with an explanation before
    either phone rings.
  - Include Messages in Backups, on by default: turn it off to keep this
    device's messages and files out of iCloud and computer backups. A
    restored device then gets its history back from the server, but
    can't read encrypted messages from before.
- Invitations to a group now wait for your answer, with a notification,
  instead of joining the group straight away.

## Changed

- On the Mac, a message's quick actions now show only while the pointer
  is on the message itself, not anywhere across its row, and the row no
  longer lights up under the pointer.
- On the Mac, the Dock icon shows a dot when only groups that don't
  notify you have unread messages, like their dot in the chat list. A
  count of messages that notify still takes its place.
- The chat list no longer draws branches and birds behind the chats; its
  soft colour wash stays.
- Behind a chat's messages, the big branches gave way to small Yapnest
  birds and leaves of a few kinds, spread evenly.
- In Settings and a chat's or group's settings, every row's icon is now
  mauve and its text black or white, or red for one that removes
  something. A chat's Call, Video and Search buttons sit a little further
  from its name and closer to the settings below.
- A picture sent as a file now loses its camera details (make, model,
  serial, time taken, software, owner) as well as its location.
- Someone's photo is fetched only once you accept their contact request,
  so a stranger's server doesn't learn you're online from the request.
- Signing out also deletes the diagnostic log.
- Calls, files, automatic picture downloads, receipts, typing and group
  invitations now come only from people you added or approved. Some
  servers, such as Openfire, put a stranger in your contacts without
  asking you, and that was enough before.
- On the Mac, only pictures, videos, sound and PDFs received as files
  open in their app; anything else opens in a preview. A file's kind is
  now judged from its name, never from what the sender says it is, and
  invisible characters that can disguise a file's name are removed.

## Fixed

- On the Mac, messages arriving in the open chat while its window can't
  be seen, such as with the screen locked or the window on another
  desktop, now stay unread until you see them.
- New private groups on ejabberd servers no longer turn on room
  logging, which can publish a group's conversation as a web page.
- A link to a page made to slow down its preview no longer holds the app
  up.
