# Yapnest 1.0 (6)

## Changed

- A group no longer says "Joining…" each time it opens: Yapnest joins it
  again quietly, and says so only when that takes more than a few seconds.
- Settings and a chat's settings keep their Done button on the screens
  they open, so closing them no longer takes going back first.
- On the Mac, the account at the foot of the chat list shows your status:
  a dot for your availability, and your status message beneath your name.

## Fixed

- On the Mac, Shift-Return starts a new line instead of sending.
- Messages to people who aren't your contacts no longer say you're active
  in the chat, as typing notifications already didn't.
- A picture sent as a file loses its location, as a photo does.
- An upload that the server redirects is checked like a download, so it
  can't be sent into your local network.
- A contact's picture on the call screen is sharp instead of blurred.
- Sharing to a chat while Yapnest is open says it was sent, rather than
  that it will be sent the next time Yapnest opens.
- A message can't make words look highlighted in search results.
- On the iPhone, a chat's name is no longer cut short for a moment as the
  chat opens.
- On the Mac, the pointer turns into a hand over all of a chat's header,
  not just its status line. The chat list now stays on screen, as hiding
  it could leave no way to bring it back.
