# Yapnest 1.0 (5)

## New

- Settings sync between your devices: link previews, GIFs, read markers,
  typing notifications, last seen, your status, and each chat's pin,
  archive, link previews and notification sound. Favourite GIFs and your
  most used emoji follow you too. They're kept in a private node on your
  own server that only your account can read.
- Voice messages: hold the microphone to record, tap to lock, slide away to
  cancel. They play in the chat with a waveform.
- Text files dropped or shared into a chat are attached as files, and a
  paste of more than 8,000 characters is sent as a .txt file.
- Settings > Export Logs saves a diagnostics log to send with a bug report.
  It holds no messages, contacts or addresses.
- A chat's settings show the contact's address.

## Changed

- Editing or deleting a message works offline. The change shows at once
  and is sent when you're back online, retried until the server has it.
- An encrypted file is uploaded under a random name, so your upload server
  doesn't learn what it was called. The people you send it to see the
  random name too.
- Large files are uploaded without being held in memory several times
  over, so big videos no longer risk running the app out of memory.
- A call keeps the connection open while the app is in the background.

## Fixed

- An edit or deletion that arrived before the message it changes, as
  happens when restoring history, was lost. It is now applied when the
  message arrives.
- Restoring history after a reinstall retries the chats that failed,
  instead of stopping.
- A device marked compromised could lose that mark.
- Notifications for edited messages, and several pushes arriving at once,
  could show twice or not update.
- Link previews that failed while offline weren't retried, in groups
  especially.
- A deleted message could show as deleted twice, and marking a chat
  unread could land on a deleted message.
- Playing a voice message while recording one is refused instead of
  cutting the recording off.
- GIFs with impossible sizes, and reactions on messages without an id,
  are handled instead of failing.
- Sharing to a chat while the app is busy queues the message instead of
  failing.
